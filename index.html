Account Deletion & Privacy Requests

In alignment with data safety regulations and application store policies, Camargos World Inc. provides users with a transparent right to delete their profiles and all linked data asset histories.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to permanently purge your user profile, follow these steps:

  • Send a direct email request from your registered address to: info@camargosworld.com
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Provide your app account username or registered email identity inside the message body.

What Data is Permanently Purged?

Upon verification and execution of your request, the following information will be completely scrubbed from our systems:

  • Personal Identity Profiles: Names, email addresses, and secure authentication metadata tokens.
  • Application Interaction Data: Core preferences, configurations, saved settings, and local usage logs.

Data Retention Window

Wiping procedures are manually validated and fulfilled within 7 business days. Please note that once completed, this operation cannot be undone. We do not retain data post-deletion, except where strictly required to comply with native legal, financial, or regulatory transaction logging requirements.
