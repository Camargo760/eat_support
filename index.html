App Support

Last updated: 26/09/2026

This page explains how to request technical support and troubleshooting assistance for your E.A.T. (Everything Around Town) application, whether you're a customer or a vendor.

How to request support

1

Email us your support request

Send an email from your active address to our team using the address listed below.

2

Include app identity & system details

Provide the error messages, device details, and whether you are logged in as a customer or vendor storefront.

3

We evaluate and resolve your ticket

We will review your system diagnostics and reply with a resolution path or technical fix updates.

info@camargosworld.com

Use the subject line "App Support Request" so it reaches us quickly.

What to include in your request

To help us isolate application bugs or server synchronization errors quickly, please include:

  • The **full name** and registered **email address** associated with your app profile
  • Your operating platform specifics (e.g., Apple iOS 17, Android 14)
  • Whether your profile type is a **customer** or a **vendor**
  • A detailed description or screenshot details of the software crash or behavior window
Technical logs and identifiers shared are strictly utilized to execute software quality solutions — review our main policy agreements for global handling scopes.

Technical Response Windows

Here is how our technical operations queue handles response windows for service updates:

Service Scope Target Processing Time
Initial Diagnostic Ticket Review Evaluated and acknowledged within 2 business days of submission logs.
Minor App / Account Bug Resolution Patched or cleared internally within 5 business days of verification.
Critical Infrastructure Outages Escalated immediately; status updates provided within 24 hours until resolved.
