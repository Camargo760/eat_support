Welcome to the support center for **E.A.T. (Everything Around Town)**. Whether you are a customer searching for local dining options or a vendor managing your digital storefront, we are here to ensure a smooth technical experience.

How to request support

1

Email us your support request

2

3

We address and resolve your ticket

Our team will look into your diagnostic details and respond with a solution path or follow-up troubleshooting steps.
